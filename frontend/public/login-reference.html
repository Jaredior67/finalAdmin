About FixIT

FixIT (Facility Issue eXpress and Integrated Tracking System) replaces scattered messages and paper slips with one tracked request for every facility concern on campus. It is run with the Planning, Development, and Auxiliary Services office of ISAT U Dumangas Campus.

Who uses it

Faculty and staffReport broken, unsafe or missing facilities and follow the status.
ApproversReview requests, set priority and release them for repair.
MaintenanceReceive assigned jobs, update progress and mark work done.
AdministratorsManage accounts, facilities and campus-wide reports.

Services

Everything a facility request needs, from the first report to the final record.

Issue reportingFile a concern with location, details and a photo in a minute.
Live status trackingSee whether a request is pending, approved, assigned or done.
Approval workflowRequests reach the right approver before work begins.
Job assignmentMaintenance staff are assigned and can report progress.
NotificationsGet alerts when your request changes status.
Reports and historyReview past repairs by building, room or date.
